16 single-chain de novo binders (55-74 aa) against domain III of the human
EGFR ectodomain: 8 carrying an engineered histidine network intended to make
binding pH-dependent, 1 that arrived with such a network already in place and
is submitted unchanged, and 7 unmodified parents included as a fallback should
the switch fail. Ranked top-first in the CSV.

APPROACH

Every residue of the extracellular region was annotated on four layers:
human/mouse conservation, solvent accessibility, N-glycan occlusion, and
disulfide architecture. A residue was designable only if exposed above 0.2
relative SASA, clear of glycan, not disulfide-bonded, and *identical* between
human and mouse. 168 of 621 qualified. The species criterion is absolute
rather than conservative because the cetuximab epitope is 20 of 27 residues
identical with none differing outright, and cetuximab still does not recognise
murine EGFR.

6ARU is numbered 24 below UniProt. The offset was verified independently of
the alignment that produced it: mapping UniProt's 25 annotated disulfide pairs
through it placed 24 of them at 2.03-2.05 A SG-SG in the crystal.

Binders were generated with BindCraft against a 204-residue domain III trim.
Histidines were then placed on the resulting interfaces, since BindCraft
optimises AlphaFold2 confidence and has no notion of pH.

WHAT IS DISTINCTIVE

Wyman linkage caps the pH dependence of binding at 1.23 kcal/mol per fully
coupled protonation across pH 7.4 to 6.5. One histidine therefore buys about
eightfold, which is not the requested behaviour, so the design families were
organised around the *number* of coupled protonation events rather than around
any single position.

The reach of a histidine was measured rather than assumed. An earlier version
allowed the CB to sit 4-10 A from the target handle; a rotamer feasibility test
then vetoed 33 of 33 proposals, every one for failing to reach. Across the 23
histidines resolved in 6ARU, CB-ND1 is 2.53 A and CB-NE2 is 3.67 A, giving a
ceiling near 7.7 A. The reported selectivity of the best variant fell from
86-fold to 55-fold once the bound was corrected. The earlier figure was an
artefact of proposing contacts no rotamer can form.

Placement was tested against a null. 3000 random draws per design were taken
from exactly the pool the method could have chosen from - interface, exposed,
substitutable - removing only the targeting. Designed placements average 1.82
kcal/mol against 0.40 for random; 5 of 8 reach p < 0.05. No designed variant
exceeds the maximum of its own null, so the claim is about frequency, and the
p-values are uncorrected.

Every variant and its unmodified parent were re-predicted under identical
settings. Damage does not track substitution count: the lead variant engages
three handles and changes i_pTM by +0.002, while one single substitution
collapsed its complex from 0.819 to 0.243 and was excluded.

RANKING

Variants whose substitutions destroyed the interface were excluded. The rest
are ordered by estimated coupling, then by whether the interface survived
intact rather than merely tolerably, then by the control p-value, then by
composition risk. Parents follow the variants.

LIMITATIONS

The coupling estimates are model-dependent upper bounds under an assumed
2.0-unit pKa shift on binding. That shift is the quantity that decides the
outcome and cannot be computed before the complex exists; PROPKA's error is
near one pKa unit, comparable to the effects being reasoned about.

These binders were generated by AlphaFold2 and re-predicted by AlphaFold2, so
absolute confidence values are not independent evidence of binding. The
variant-versus-parent comparison is sound because neither was optimised and
both are predicted identically, but only the assay can establish binding.

Every design is strongly acidic, with glutamate between 14 and 28 percent
against a natural average near 7. The most extreme case is the design carrying
the lead variant. This is an expression and solubility liability across the
whole portfolio. It is weighed in the ranking but not solved.

No structural novelty comparison (e.g. Foldseek) was run; no design returns a
hit from the RCSB sequence service, and the highest pairwise identity within
the portfolio is 27%.
